Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Internal Family Systems (IFS) is an evidence-based model of inner work that understands us as having many different “parts” — like inner voices, roles, or aspects of our personality — and a deeper, steady core called Self.

Rather than seeing these parts as problems, IFS views them as protective and adaptive, each carrying its own history, burdens, and intentions to help. Some parts become loud and reactive, some shut down, some carry old pain, and some quietly hold our creativity, playfulness, and wisdom.

In IFS, we don’t force change or try to “get rid of” any part. Instead, we create space for a compassionate, curious relationship with your inner system. As more Self-leadership emerges — with qualities like calm, clarity, courage, and connection — parts can relax, heal, and take on new roles that serve you in the present.

I like to think of us as having as many parts as a tree has leaves: each one distinct, each with its own story, all belonging to one living, connected whole. IFS offers a way to listen to these parts with more respect and less fear, so your inner world can feel more integrated, grounded, and aligned with who you truly are.
